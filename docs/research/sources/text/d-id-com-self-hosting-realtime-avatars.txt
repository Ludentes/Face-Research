FAQ

Self-hosting, answered.

Does any of my data leave my environment?

No. Conversations, avatar configurations, and assets all stay inside your own network, and the avatar runs there too. The only outbound traffic goes to the speech, language, and voice providers you choose to connect. D-ID never receives your end-user data while an avatar is running.

Can I bring my own models and voices?

Yes. The pipeline is provider-agnostic: plug in the speech-to-text, language model, and text-to-speech services you prefer. You stay in control of cost, quality, and where each call is processed.

Can I use my own avatars?

Absolutely. Use your own custom avatars for a fully branded presence, or choose from D-ID’s library of stock presenters to get started quickly.

What infrastructure do I need?

You run it in your own cloud account with GPU capacity for the avatar rendering plus standard compute for the streaming backend. It’s cloud-agnostic across Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud, and transactable through the Azure and AWS marketplaces. Our team helps you size everything during onboarding.

How does it scale?

There’s no cap on concurrent sessions. The deployment scales automatically based on the metrics you define, adding and removing capacity as demand changes, to as many simultaneous avatar conversations as your infrastructure supports, with high-availability options for production.

What support do I get?

D-ID’s forward-deployed engineers help you design, deploy, and optimize the stack, and you receive ongoing security patches, bug fixes, and direct access to our experts. Book a demo and we’ll map out a deployment plan with you.
