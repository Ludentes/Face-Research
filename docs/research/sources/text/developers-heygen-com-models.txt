HeyGen Video

HeyGen Video
generates video from a prompt, inventing the subject, the setting and the sound together in one call to
POST /v3/models/videos
.

​
HeyGen Avatar

Avatar rendering engines turn a look and a script into video, selected per request through the
engine
field on
POST /v3/videos
. New integrations should default to
Avatar IV
for broad coverage, opt into
Avatar V
for the highest-fidelity motion and lip-sync, and use
Avatar III
for its dedicated photo-to-video pipeline.
