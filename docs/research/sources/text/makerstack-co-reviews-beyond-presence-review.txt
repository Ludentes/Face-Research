What is Beyond Presence?

Beyond Presence is a Munich-based startup that builds real-time
AI avatar
APIs. Founded in 2024 by the team behind Presize (a computer vision company Meta acquired in 2022), the platform converts audio streams into lifelike video avatars with frame-accurate lip sync, facial expressions, and emotional responses. It raised $3.1 million in pre-seed funding from HV Capital, with angel investors from Meta, DeepMind, and Zalando.

The product is API-first, targeting developers and technical teams rather than end users. Two core APIs power everything: Speech-to-Video (S2V) for converting audio to avatar video, and Managed Agents for end-to-end conversational AI agents with integrated voice, vision, and memory. Output is 1080p at 35 FPS, which is a significant step up from the choppy, low-res avatars that defined earlier generations of this technology.

Key Features

Genesis 1.0 Avatar Model

Beyond Presence’s proprietary avatar model delivers sub-100ms latency for the Speech-to-Video API. The avatars
render
at 1080p/35fps with precise lip sync and natural facial expressions. Nine stock avatars are available, plus custom avatar creation from your own likeness. The visual quality is noticeably better than most competitors in the real-time avatar space, though still not indistinguishable from real humans.

Speech-to-Video API

The S2V API converts any audio input into synchronized avatar video in real time. It supports any language through third-party TTS providers including
ElevenLabs
, OpenAI,
Cartesia
, and Hume. This flexibility means you are not locked into Beyond Presence’s voice ecosystem. The API consumes 50 credits per minute and integrates with
LiveKit
for WebRTC streaming.

Managed Agents API

For teams that want the full stack without building from scratch, the Managed Agents API provides conversational AI agents with integrated voice, vision, and memory. Global latency is approximately 1.2 seconds end-to-end. These agents can handle autonomous customer interactions, interviews, and training sessions. The API consumes 100 credits per minute, double the S2V rate.

Developer Integration Suite

Beyond Presence integrates with LiveKit,
PipeCat
, and
n8n
. Python and JavaScript SDKs are available, plus iFrame embedding for quick prototyping. The documentation is clean and the developer experience is polished for a pre-seed company. Enterprise customers can deploy on-premises for data sovereignty compliance.

Who Should Use Beyond Presence

Beyond Presence is built for developers and technical teams integrating avatar experiences into existing products. HR tech companies building AI interviewers,
customer support
platforms creating 24/7 video agents, e-learning companies developing interactive tutors, and sales teams automating demo presentations are the primary use cases.

This is not a drag-and-drop tool for non-technical users. You need development resources to integrate the APIs. If you want pre-built avatar videos (not real-time conversation), tools like Synthesia or HeyGen are more appropriate.

Where Beyond Presence Falls Short

The company is extremely early-stage. Founded in 2024 with $3.1 million in pre-seed funding, there is inherent risk in building critical infrastructure on a startup this young. The 1,000+ client claim is encouraging but unverifiable, and pre-seed companies can pivot or shut down.

Nine stock avatars is a small library. Custom avatar creation helps, but competitors like HeyGen and Synthesia offer 100+ stock avatars. For teams that need variety without creating custom avatars, the selection feels limited.

The Managed Agents API’s 1.2-second global latency is noticeable in conversation. While the S2V API delivers sub-100ms, the full agent pipeline adds delay that can make interactions feel slightly unnatural. This matters for use cases like customer support where responsiveness is critical.

Alternatives

Tavus

Tavus is the closest competitor in the real-time conversational avatar space. It specializes in conversational video interfaces with digital twins and real-time interaction. Tavus focuses more on personalized video at scale, while Beyond Presence emphasizes API flexibility and developer experience.

Synthesia

Synthesia is the market leader in AI video generation but focuses on scripted, presentation-style avatar videos rather than real-time conversation. It is better for training videos and corporate communications. Not a direct competitor for real-time conversational use cases.

D-ID

D-ID offers both pre-recorded and real-time avatar capabilities through its Creative Reality Studio. It has a broader feature set for general avatar creation but less specialized in the real-time conversational niche that Beyond Presence targets.

FAQ

How realistic are Beyond Presence avatars?

The Genesis 1.0 model produces 1080p avatars at 35 FPS with accurate lip sync and facial expressions. They are among the most realistic real-time avatars available, though still identifiable as AI-generated upon close inspection. The quality is significantly better than low-latency competitors.

Can I create a custom avatar from my own likeness?

Yes. Beyond Presence supports custom avatar creation in addition to its 9 stock avatars. Custom avatars use your likeness for branded or personal avatar experiences. The creation process and timeline vary by plan.

What languages does Beyond Presence support?

Beyond Presence supports any language through integration with third-party TTS providers like ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Cartesia, and Hume. The avatar lip sync adapts to any language audio input, so you are limited only by your TTS provider’s language support.

Is Beyond Presence GDPR compliant?

Yes. Beyond Presence is a German company (GmbH) and is GDPR compliant, SOC 2 certified, and EU AI Act aligned. Enterprise customers can deploy on-premises for full data sovereignty. Servers are hosted with 99%+ uptime SLA.
