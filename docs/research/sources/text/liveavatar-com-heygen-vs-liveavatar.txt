At a Glance
HeyGen

creates pre-recorded AI videos: you write a script, pick an avatar, and get a finished video. Think marketing videos, training content, and personalized outreach at scale.
LiveAvatar

offers an API that powers real-time, interactive avatar conversations: a human-like face that talks with your users live, powered by AI. Think customer support bots, sales agents, tutors, and AI assistants that users can actually speak to.
Different products. Different pricing. Different accounts. Same parent company.

Three Things to Remember
1
.
Separate Products
HeyGen and LiveAvatar are built by the same company but are distinct products with their own apps, documentation, and roadmaps.
2
.
Separate Billing
Each product has its own pricing plans and credit system. Credits do not transfer between HeyGen and LiveAvatar. You need a subscription on each.
3
.
Same Login
You sign into LiveAvatar using your HeyGen email ("Sign in with HeyGen"), but that’s where the connection ends. Once inside, LiveAvatar has its own workspace, avatars, and credits.
