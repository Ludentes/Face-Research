Introducing Avatar IV, a faster and simpler way to create lifelike videos using your HeyGen avatar.

Now with AvatarIV and just a few clicks, you can generate quick avatar-led videos, perfect for real-time intros, updates, or replies. It’s the fastest way to bring your digital self or character to life, now available for all users!

What Makes Avatar IV Different?
Advance Intonation & Emotion:
Unlike traditional avatars that simply sync to your words, this new-to-the-world Avatar model actually
interprets
them. Built on a diffusion-inspired audio-to-expression engine, it analyzes your vocal tone, rhythm, and emotion to generate photorealistic facial movements with true-to-life timing. Think head tilts, natural pauses, subtle cadences, and micro-expressions—all from a single image.
​
​
​Speed + Simplicity:
No complex script writing, scene setup, or editing in the Studio. Just open Avatar IV, fill out the required fields, and your video is ready in seconds. It’s designed for instant, on-the-fly communication.
​
​
Great with non-human faces and photos:
AvatarIV is the best suited engine for bringing to life avatars that are cartoonish, animated, or don't have traditional human-like features!

Where Can You Use AvatarIV on HeyGen

three different ways
:
1. Photo to Video Tool is Now Accessible Directly From Your Home Page!
Bringing your photos to life has never been easier. HeyGen has streamlined its interface, allowing you to transform any image into a talking avatar in just a few clicks, directly from your main dashboard.

What’s New?
No more digging through complex menus. You can now find the
"Photo to Video"
tag directly on your Home Page. This integration is designed to speed up the workflow for creators, marketers, and businesses alike.

You can access this feature from the
Tools tab
on the left side bar, under
All Apps.

upload your photo
or use one from our library.

Then either
write your script
or
upload an audio file
. If you're using a written script,
select your preferred voice
. Once everything’s set, click
Generate
to create your video. It really is that simple!

Photo-to-video is perfect for animating
non-human photos

2. AvatarIV in Studio Experience
This option allows you to create full-length videos with your avatar powered by the AvatarIV engine.
AvatarIV engine is ideal for avatars build from photos, especially effective for
non-human, cartoonish, or 3D model avatars
.

Credit Usage Details
Avatar IV uses credits at different rates depending on the Look type:
​
Photo Look:
16 credits per minute
​
Video Look:
31 credits per minute
​
On legacy unlimited plans:
Avatar IV costs 16 Premium Credits/min (Photo Look) or 31 Premium Credits/min (Video Look). Avatar III videos remain free and unlimited on legacy plans.
Photo-to-Video
Custom motion prompt / Higher quality with Custom Motion Prompt:
credits are consumed at a
2:1 ratio
(e.g. a 30s video = 20 Credits used for AvatarIV).

Studio Experience
Usage here depends on the
script/audio length
and how much of your video requires avatar generation. You can actually limit AvatarIV usage only to specific scenes to save on your Credits cost.

Example: a 30s video might only use 20 or 25 seconds of credits, depending on pauses or silent moments.
✨Fun fact: we only charge for the
actual seconds of avatar generation
, not the entire video length — so you get more value out of each credit!

🛎️ You cannot create Photo-to-video videos longer than 5000 characters in terms of your script length.

AvatarIV now available on API !
You can now use API credits to create videos with AvatarIV!
Generally, 1 minute of AvatarIV video via API should cost about $4 at 1080p. Avatar IV does not support native 4K via the API: a 4K request is accepted but is an upscaled 1080p render, and is billed at the 1080p rate. This does not affect Avatar IV videos created in HeyGen Studio. Please review the full
API pricing here.
