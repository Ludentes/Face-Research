Frequently asked questions
Quick answers about UneeQ and how teams use Immersive Training Platform.
Who is UneeQ?
UneeQ is a training company that designs lifelike roleplay for enterprise teams. Its Immersive Training Platform enables employees to practice real workplace conversations face to face with an AI digital human, then get instant AI coaching.
What is a digital human?
A digital human is a lifelike, AI-powered character that talks with people face to face in real time. It responds with facial expressions, gestures, and tone of voice, so the conversation feels like talking to a real person.
How is UneeQ different from voice-only AI roleplay tools?
Voice-only tools can only work with what learners say. UneeQ's digital humans are face to face, so learners practice reading the room: expressions, emotions, and body language. They hold tough personas for the whole conversation, and our in-depth performance analytics measure how people show up, including the non-verbal signals.
What can teams practice?
Any conversation where the stakes are high, from sales discovery and objection handling to escalated customers, performance reviews, tough feedback, and job interviews. Teams use UneeQ for sales training, customer service training, leadership training, and education. You can also build your own scenarios from your playbooks and training manuals.
