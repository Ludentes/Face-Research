HeyGen vs LiveAvatar - What's the Difference?

vs

Two products, one company. Built for different jobs. Here's when to use each.

AI Video CreationReal-time Conversational Avatars

At a Glance

HeyGen creates pre-recorded AI videos: you write a script, pick an avatar, and get a finished video. Think marketing videos, training content, and personalized outreach at scale.

LiveAvatar offers an API that powers real-time, interactive avatar conversations: a human-like face that talks with your users live, powered by AI. Think customer support bots, sales agents, tutors, and AI assistants that users can actually speak to.

Different products. Different pricing. Different accounts. Same parent company.

AI Video Creation Platform

  • •Generate polished AI videos from text scripts
  • •100+ stock avatars or create your own digital twin
  • •40+ languages with lip-sync translation
  • •Video templates, batch generation, personalized videos
  • •Output: permanent, downloadable MP4 video files
  • •No coding required - browser-based studio

Real-time Conversational Avatar API

  • •Live, two-way conversations with an AI avatar
  • •User speaks → avatar listens, thinks, and responds in real-time
  • •Embed on your website or integrate via API/SDK
  • •Output: ephemeral, generated-on-the-fly video + audio streamed via WebRTC (not saved)
  • •Developer-first - API, SDK, or iframe embed

Three Things to Remember

1. Separate Products

HeyGen and LiveAvatar are built by the same company but are distinct products with their own apps, documentation, and roadmaps.

2. Separate Billing

Each product has its own pricing plans and credit system. Credits do not transfer between HeyGen and LiveAvatar. You need a subscription on each.

3. Same Login

You sign into LiveAvatar using your HeyGen email ("Sign in with HeyGen"), but that’s where the connection ends. Once inside, LiveAvatar has its own workspace, avatars, and credits.
