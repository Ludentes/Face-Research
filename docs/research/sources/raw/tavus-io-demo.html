Three ways to work with us

Build it, embed it, or let us handle the whole thing.

Solution #1

White-labeled APIs

Give your users a PAL that carries your brand, not ours. The same three models behind every PAL run inside your product with no Tavus branding: Phoenix-4.5 renders the whole PAL in real time; Raven-1 reads tone, expression, and intent; and Sparrow-2 knows when to speak, wait, or listen.

Solution #2

Wherever the conversation happens

A PAL can live inside your app as a native, face-to-face experience, sit on your website, or go out as a link to candidates, patients, or your own team. It takes real actions through tool calling, and inside your product it can bring up a chart, a form, or a calendar mid-conversation with Magic Canvas.

Solution #3

Fully managed

Your team focuses on results while ours handles the rest: persona design, conversation architecture, integrations, and ongoing tuning, from kickoff through production. Native connections to your CRM, ATS, EHR, LMS, and scheduling systems send the right data to the right place.

What is a PAL?

Every piece of software asks people to translate what they mean into its terms before it can help. A PAL takes that step away. It's a new kind of application you talk to rather than operate: it sees and hears the person on the other side, answers in real time with a face, a voice, and a personality of its own, and remembers them every time they talk. A PAL can:

‍

  • Hold thousands of face-to-face conversations at once, each with the same judgment
  • Coach, onboard, and support people face to face
  • Work around the clock, across time zones and languages
  • Read tone, expression, and intent, and adjust as the conversation moves
  • Build trust and rapport, not just efficiency
