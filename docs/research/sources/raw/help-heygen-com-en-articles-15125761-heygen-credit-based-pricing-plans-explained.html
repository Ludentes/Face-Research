HeyGen Credit-Based Pricing Plans & Subscriptions Explained

Here you can find all the up-to-date information on HeyGen’s credit-based subscriptions and pricing.

HeyGen is moving to credit-based plans to make pricing clearer, easier to understand, and more flexible as new AI video features become available. Instead of mixing unlimited usage with Premium Credit pool, credits now work as the main usage system across paid plans.

This article covers HeyGen’s credit-based Avatar & Video subscriptions. These are separate from API credits (pay-as-you-go) and LiveAvatar plans.

Click here to learn about our API credits to generate videos via API, or here to learn about our LiveAvatar pricing.

This article is for the newer credit-based plans. If you cancel your legacy plan or switch to a credit-based plan, you will not be able to switch back to the old legacy plan. Note: Legacy Premium Credits now match the rates in this article for paid features (Avatar IV, Avatar V, Video Translation, etc.). Avatar III videos and audio dubbing (no lip-sync translation) remain free and unlimited on legacy plans.

Credit Rollover

Unused credits may roll over depending on your billing type.

Monthly subscribers:

  • Unused credits roll over for one additional billing cycle.

  • For example, if you do not use all of your credits this month, the remaining eligible credits can carry into the next month.

Annual subscribers:

  • Annual subscription does not provide all the annual credits at once. You receive monthly allocation of your credits, at the monthly billing date anniversary when you purchased your plan. For example, if you purchased your annual plan on January 17th, your credits will be recharged every 17th of that month.

  • Unused credits accumulate until your annual renewal date.

If you cancel your paid plan and move to Free, unused credits do not roll over.

🛎️ Credits are used in order of expiration. HeyGen always deducts the credits closest to expiring first. So if you have gifted credits alongside your rollover credits, the ones with the earlier expiration date are applied to your next video — no credit goes to waste unnecessarily.

Add-Ons

Business Credit Add-Ons

Business users can purchase additional credits if they need more than their included monthly credit amount.

Additional Business credits:

  • Cost $0.05 per credit

  • Minimum purchase: $5 for 100 credits

  • Credits are purchased in 100-credit blocks

  • Use the + and - buttons in the add-ons flow to increase or decrease the number of packs

  • Auto-reload is only available for Business users

Creator and Pro Credit Add-Ons

Creator and Pro do not use one-time credit packs.

  • Creator users who need more credits can upgrade to Pro.

  • Pro users who need more credits can upgrade to a higher Pro tier.

This makes the credit amount part of the plan itself, instead of a separate recurring credit pack.

Avatar Add-Ons

Need more avatars than your plan includes? You can add more avatar slots with flexible monthly or yearly plans.

Included Digital Twin slots can vary by account creation date and region. Creator and Pro accounts include either 1 or 5 slots, depending on region and account creation date. Business accounts include 5 slots by default, or 10 in some regions. You can see the slots on your account on the Avatars page. If you need more, you can add slots at any time as an add-on, and add-on pricing may also vary by account creation date and region.

Add-On Pricing:

  • Video Avatar Slots: $29/month or $288/year

  • LiveAvatar Slots: $49/month — only available on the LiveAvatar platform

To access Add-Ons, go to the pricing screen, scroll down to Avatar Add-On, and click Purchase more avatars now. From there, you can select as many Video Avatar slots as you want. Use the plus and minus signs to adjust the quantity before heading to checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do credits work?

Credits are used for video generation and asset creation on HeyGen credit-based paid plans. Different features cost different amounts of credits. Some features charge per minute of output video, while others charge a fixed amount per generation.

Do Free users have credits?

No. Free users do not use credits. Free users can create 1–3 free videos depending on their region.

What happens if I run out of credits?

It depends on your plan:

  • Creator users can upgrade to Pro for more credits.

  • Pro users can upgrade to a higher Pro tier with more credits.

  • Business users can buy additional credits in 100-credit blocks, starting at $5 for 100 credits, or turn on auto-reload.

Can I buy more credits on Creator?

No. Creator does not support one-time credit purchases. If you need more credits, you can upgrade to Pro.

Can I buy more credits on Pro?

Pro users can get more credits by upgrading to a higher Pro tier. Pro does not use one-time credit packs.

Can I buy more credits on Business?

Yes. Business users can purchase extra credits for $0.05 per credit. The minimum purchase is $5 for 100 credits, and you can increase the purchase in $5 / 100-credit increments using the + and - buttons in the add-ons flow.

Business users can also enable auto-reload so additional credits are added automatically when the balance runs low.

Do unused credits roll over?

Yes, depending on your billing type.

  • Monthly subscribers: unused credits roll over for one additional billing cycle.

  • Annual subscribers: unused credits accumulate until the annual renewal date.

If you cancel your paid plan and move to Free, unused credits do not roll over.

What happens if I upgrade my plan mid-cycle?

When you upgrade to a higher plan or Pro tier mid-cycle, you’ll be charged the difference and receive the additional credits right away. You will receive your credits immediately and be charged the difference. These credits will roll over to the next cycle as well, as long as you keep your paid plan.
​
Example, if you purchase Creator with 600 credits for $29 on 1/1, and choose to upgrade to Pro $49 and 1000 credits on 1/20, you will be charged the $20 difference and received the 400 credit difference on the 1/20, to be applied until the end of next billing cycle on 3/1.

What happens if I downgrade my plan?

Downgrades usually take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, so you can continue using your current plan until the end of the billing period.

If you downgrade to a lower paid monthly plan, eligible unused credits can roll over for one additional billing cycle. If you cancel and move to Free, unused credits do not roll over.

How do I cancel my plan / subscription?

To cancel your plan review this article or the text below

  • Log in to your HeyGen account.

  • Go to Settings > Plan & Billing.

  • Next to the upgrade button, click the down arrow.

  • Choose Cancel your plan.

️ Note: Pausing your plan does not cancel it entirely—it simply postpones the next charge by 30 days from your last billing cycle date. If you'd like to pause indefinitely, we recommend canceling your account. All of your avatars, videos, and projects will still be accessible when you're ready to sign back up.

How do I upgrade my plan?

You can upgrade your plan anytime through your Plan & Billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately as you will be charged the difference in pricing and receive the additional credits difference immediately based on the plan change.

Can I switch back to my old legacy unlimited plan?

No. If you cancel your legacy unlimited plan or switch to a credit-based plan, you will not be able to switch back to the old legacy plan.

I’m on a legacy unlimited plan. Will my plan change automatically?

No. If you are already on a legacy paid plan, you can keep your current plan as long as your subscription remains active and in good standing. This article is for credit-based plans.

How do I apply a promo code or discount to my purchase?

If you’ve received a promo or discount code, you can apply it at checkout. Look for the Add promotion code field under the subtotal before you complete your purchase. We occasionally share codes like these, so be sure to follow us on social media to catch future deals.
