What is an
interactive avatar?

An interactive avatar, also called a real-time avatar, is a digital character that can listen, talk, and respond to users in real time within a video call. Unlike static video avatars that play pre-recorded clips, LemonSlice's interactive avatars generate every frame on the fly so the lips, facial expressions, and body language all move naturally. Whether you need a photorealistic AI assistant for customer support or a cartoon mascot for education, any character with a face can become an interactive AI avatar in minutes.
