Frequently asked questions about AI video.

Can I use Synthesia for free?

The Basic plan is free and includes allowances for limited video creation, dubbing and survey responses.

How can I test Synthesia?

Free AI video: You can generate an AI video for free here.

Free sales demo: If you're interested in testing Synthesia for a larger Enterprise, you can book a personal demo with our sales team here.

How much does Synthesia cost?

Basic is free. Starter and Pro prices depend on whether you pay monthly or annually - the plan cards above show the price. Pro is available at several credit levels, so you can pick the credit volume that best suits you need. Enterprise pricing is custom.

What happens if I go over my plan's monthly limit?

Video creation and survey response collection pause until your plan renews. To keep going straight away, you can buy extra credits from Plan & billing, or move to a plan with more credits - on Pro you can simply choose a higher credit level.

Do unused video minutes roll over into the next month?

No. Your plan's monthly allowance and credits reset each billing period. On annual plans the year's credits are available upfront. Credits you buy separately are valid for 12 months.

How and when are credits consumed?

Credits are used when something is generated, never while you're editing or previewing. What each product uses:

  • Video creation - based on the avatar footage generated, not the length of the finished video. A typical finished minute uses about 100 credits, more if the avatar is on screen throughout. We only count new footage: edit three seconds of an existing video and regenerate, and you're charged for three seconds.
  • Dubbing - per minute of video dubbed: 80 credits with lip-sync, 40 without. Your 10 included minutes each month can be used for dubbing as well as video creation.
  • Surveys - 350 credits per response, after the 3 responses included every month. Building and publishing a survey is free.
  • B-roll - clips and images you generate in the AI Playground or Media Library are charged per asset; the cost depends on the model and is shown before you generate.
  • Interactive Avatars (API) - per minute of conversation: 10 credits for the avatar only, 20 with the full widget.

Can I upgrade my account at any time?

Yes. Change plans, switch to annual billing or adjust your Pro credit level any time from Settings → Plan & billing. For Enterprise, contact our sales team.

Can I cancel Synthesia at any time?

Yes. Simply go to your account settings in Synthesia and cancel your plan.

Is Synthesia a one-time payment?

No, the payment for using Synthesia is recurring. For Starter and Pro plan users, the billing cycle can be monthly or annual.

Can I pay per video?

No. Synthesia is based on monthly and yearly subscriptions.

How much is a custom avatar?

Personal avatars are included with paid plans and custom volume on Enterprise.

Can I see more customer reviews of Synthesia?

Yes, of course. We've been selected as a High Performer on G2 and you can read the reviews here.
