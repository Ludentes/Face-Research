How to Create a Custom LiveAvatar

To start creating your avatar, you'll need to record footage specifically for this tool following the below guidelines.

⚠️ Important Note: Previous footage used for Video Avatars cannot be reused as the guidelines are unique to Custom LiveAvatar.

🛎️ Consent video: LiveAvatar Business and Enterprise plan users can upload a pre-recorded consent video. Essential and Free plan users must provide live consent via webcam, similar to the HeyGen Digital Twin live consent video.

Recording Guidelines

Make sure the footage is continuous, with no cuts or edits.

Total Duration: Minimum of 2 minutes, divided into 3 distinct parts.

  1. Listening (15 seconds) ‼️ This portion is SILENT ‼️
    ​Purpose: To show active engagement.
    Use facial expressions like raising your eyebrows or smiling to demonstrate that you're attentively listening.

  2. Talking (90 seconds)
    ​Purpose: To convey your message.
    Speak clearly and confidently, ensuring your message is concise and to the point.

  3. Idling (15 seconds) ‼️ This portion is SILENT ‼️
    ​Purpose: To show an attentive presence.
    Maintain a neutral expression, occasionally nodding without additional facial expressions.

Key Differences:

  • Listening: Engage with facial expressions to show active listening.

  • Idling: Maintain a neutral demeanor and only nod, reflecting presence without extra expressions.

Important Considerations

Background & Environment

  • Choose a clean, static background or a green screen.

  • Avoid moving objects, changing light sources, or reflections in the background.

  • If using green screen, keep it off the floor to reduce color spill.

  • Keep the subject at least 5 feet away from the backdrop to avoid shadows.

Camera Setup & Framing

  • Use a 1920x1080 HD camera or better.

  • Frame the subject chest-up; avoid showing hands unless necessary; No full-body recordings (these are not supported).

  • If hands are included (half- or full-body framing), keep them still and in resting position.

🛎️ Note: LiveAvatar currently do not support 4K output due to latency limitations.

Lighting

  • Use soft, even lighting to avoid harsh shadows.

  • Place two lights on either side of the subject with diffusion for a soft look.

  • Optionally, add a backlight above and behind the head for subject-background separation.

  • Warm up the color temperature to maintain natural skin tones.

  • If shadows appear under hands, add a light below the subject.

Performance Tips

  • Maintain steady eye contact with the camera throughout.

  • Avoid loud breathing, swaying, or unnecessary movement during pauses.

  • Keep jewelry, glasses, and shiny clothing to a minimum to ensure clean background keying.

  • Do two takes:

    • One with minimal movement.

    • One with 4–5 slow, subtle gestures (below shoulder height).

Reminder for Talent

  • Begin with an engaged listening pose.

  • Deliver the message with smooth, steady speech.

  • End with a calm idle position—no sudden movements.

Non-human or 3D Custom Avatars

Have a 3D character, photo avatar, or non-human face you'd like to turn into a LiveAvatar? Good news—you can use your own custom non-human characters to create a LiveAvatar in HeyGen!

The process is simple, but depending on the type of character, a quick manual review may be required to ensure everything works smoothly and meets our guidelines.

  1. Create your character

  2. Produce a video in they HeyGen Editing Studio that’s over 2 minutes long and includes the specific LiveAvatar footage instructions

  3. Submit the video along with a consent video of a real person

  4. The submission will be rejected automatically at first — don’t worry! Just reach out to our team at [email protected] for approval with the rejected Avatar ID [found in the top right corner under the three dot menu ... ] and a screenshot from the platform where the character was created to confirm it's free for use and doesn’t have an identifiable owner or IP restriction

  5. Once approved, you’re all set to enjoy your new LiveAvatar!

🛎️ Note: Our avatar technology is optimized for characters with distinct human features—like eyes, a nose, and lips. If your non-human character lacks these, the final avatar may not perform well in terms of realism or lip-sync accuracy.

Processing Time for Custom Avatars

Please note that your Custom LiveAvatar will take some time to be ready for use due to the tool’s complexity. To ensure the best possible result, follow the guidelines carefully and as accurately as possible.

The estimated processing time for all LiveAvatar is about ~24 hours.

How to Use A Custom Avatar

First, you'll need to tell your avatar what to say and how to say it! Since your LiveAvatar is powered by a Large Language Model (LLM), you can guide it using a prompt, which we refer to as a "context" on LiveAvatar platform, formally known as knowledge-base.

To set it up, create a Voice Agent. In the left menu under Playground, open Voice Agents, click Create New and choose Build from scratch. Give the agent a name, select your context under “Configure the brain”, then select a voice and default language under “Configure the voice”, and save. Then go to Avatars, open any public or custom avatar, choose your agent in the “Select voice agent” dropdown at the bottom of the screen, and click Chat now.

For in-depth guidance on how to write your context, please visit the Prompting your LiveAvatar article.

Users with access to Custom Avatars can also customize their LiveAvatar by changing its voice and/or renaming it.

Once you've decided on the content for your avatar and are happy with the results, you're ready to test it out! To speak with your avatar, press Chat Now.

Integrating Your LiveAvatar

You can integrate LiveAvatar into your platform in two ways- Full Mode and Lite Mode.

Full Mode – An end-to-end solution, if you want convenience. We handle the LLM, voice, and avatar layer. It’s the fastest way to integrate your LiveAvatar into your platform. It consumes 2 LiveAvatar credits per minute.

Lite Mode – Offers significantly more flexibility, if you want more control. You can bring your own LLM, voice, and add deeper customizations. We provide the avatar layer only. It consumes 1 LiveAvatar credit per minute.

🛎️ Session Data & Privacy:
• Avatar Only (Lite mode): We do not retain any session data.
• Full mode: We retain session transcripts only. You can delete them at any time via the Delete Session Events API.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

I want to add this to my site, but I can’t code. What do I do?

The LiveAvatar API has resources and documentation that will enable most web developers to add the LiveAvatar functionality to your website or product. Currently, adding LiveAvatar functionality to your site requires some programming, as there is no easy “copy-pasting” our Demo to your site with any customization.

Is there a limit on how many connections can interact with the LiveAvatar on my website at the same time?

No—LiveAvatar supports unlimited concurrent sessions on all paid plans. You pay for minutes streamed, never for concurrency. Enterprise users can access dedicated capacity and custom terms by contacting their Enterprise Representative.

I already have a custom GPT or LLM. How do I connect it to the LiveAvatar?

As mentioned before, the LiveAvatar acts as the 'body,' while the instructions come from your Context. If you want to use an existing prompt, you can copy and paste the text into a Context (only text is supported, no file uploads) and attach it to a Voice Agent. Alternatively, a developer can connect your own LLM through the LiveAvatar API (docs.liveavatar.com), using a custom LLM configuration or your own pipeline in Lite mode.

Can the LiveAvatar speak any language?

There are two parts to this answer:

First, users are not directly communicating with the LiveAvatar—they’re speaking with ChatGPT or the LLM (Large Language Model) you’ve connected. If that LLM supports multiple languages, the LiveAvatar will be able to speak those languages as well.

Second, the accent of the LiveAvatar is determined by the Voice ID you assign. HeyGen offers different Voice IDs from various text-to-speech providers, including ElevenLabs, Fish and Cartesia (OpenAI and Gemini are available when you connect your own OpenAI Realtime or Gemini setup in Lite mode). The accent and sound of the voices will vary depending on the provider.

I want my users to be able to speak with the LiveAvatar out loud. Can I do that?

Yes! You can use the LiveAvatar Web SDK in order to add voice chat functionality to your app or website.

My LiveAvatar is making weird movements. How can I fix this?

If your LiveAvatar is making strange movements, it’s likely due to the input footage. Since we can’t adjust already rendered footage, you may need to refilm. When recording footage for a LiveAvatar, it’s important to:

  • Look directly at the camera

  • Limit hand, arm, and head movements to subtle, ‘micro movements’

  • Maintain a pleasant, relaxed expression

By following these guidelines and all outlined above, you can improve the avatar’s accuracy and avoid unnatural motions.

Can I redo my LiveAvatar?

We offer one free remake of your LiveAvatar if you’re not satisfied with the final result. This is the only complimentary remake provided. For more information, please contact [email protected]

Which voice can I use with the Streaming API?

You can use different Voice IDs with Avatars. To explore the available Voice IDs, visit the AI Voices section on the HeyGen homepage.

Developers can choose from HeyGen’s Public Voices, Private Voices created alongside avatars, or voices sourced from third-party platforms like ElevenLabs.

Can I remove the background from my Custom LiveAvatar?

You can remove the background from your LiveAvatar, though this requires more advanced coding skills than most users have. For additional information, please refer to this link; however, note that our regular support center won’t be able to assist with questions on this topic.

How can I remove the watermark from the LiveAvatar?

The watermark should be removed from your LiveAvatar if you have LiveAvatar credits and are using them for streaming. LiveAvatar credits are a separate system and are not related to HeyGen API credits.

So, in general:

  • If you’re using the LiveAvatar via the HeyGen platform (Share button, hosted link, or HTML embed), the watermark cannot be removed—even with a paid plan.

  • However, if you’re using the LiveAvatar outside of HeyGen (via API token or SDK integration), then having LiveAvatar credits allows you to remove the watermark from your embedded avatar experience.

If you are seeing the watermark while using LiveAvatar credits, please reach out to [email protected] for assistance.

Where can I find the LiveAvatar trial token?

We have removed the token / secret mechanism that was formerly used with the LiveAvatar streaming process. This is not required anymore to embed LiveAvatar on your website. For developer documentation, see docs.liveavatar.com.

Can I use a Photo Avatar as a LiveAvatar?

You can create a LiveAvatar from AI photo if you provide a video (at least 2 minutes long) that follows the specified guidelines above. To do this, you can use HeyGen or another platform to create a Photo Avatar video and use a consent video featuring yourself. Note that this will trigger a moderation flag, and you’ll need to contact [email protected] for approval.
